# AI, Blockchain and Platform Economics – Working Paper Series

This repository hosts a small series of **five working papers** on the economics of
AI infrastructure, blockchain ecosystems, ride-hailing and delivery platforms,
and their comparison with “boring” but profitable FMCG businesses.

The goal is **not** to forecast prices or to give investment advice.  
The goal is to make the economics visible with compact, data-driven arguments
that founders, operators and investors can actually use.

All numbers are **order-of-magnitude estimates** based on public financial
reports and sector studies cited in each paper. Scenarios describe what
*could* happen under explicit assumptions, not what *will* necessarily happen.

---

## Structure of the repository

The project is intentionally minimal: only markdown files.

- `README.md` – overview and navigation.
- `paper1-ai-infrastructure-economics.md`  
  *Capital intensity, payback logic and profitability scenarios for AI
  infrastructure and hyperscalers.*

- `paper2-blockchain-platform-value-capture.md`  
  *How crypto exchanges and blockchain platforms make money, and why value
  capture is fragile.*

- `paper3-ride-hailing-delivery-unit-economics.md`  
  *Unit economics of ride-hailing and food-delivery platforms, with a focus
  on contribution margins and labour/regulation constraints.*

- `paper4-fmcg-vs-tech-profitability.md`  
  *A comparison between high-growth tech models and traditional FMCG/food
  companies in terms of margins, cash flow and risk.*

- `paper5-investor-founder-checklist.md`  
  *A synthetic framework and checklist for founders and investors deciding
  where to allocate scarce time and capital.*
