# AI, Blockchain and Platform Economics – Working Paper Series

This repository hosts a small series of **five working papers** on the economics of:

- AI infrastructure and enterprise adoption;
- blockchain and crypto-asset platforms;
- ride-hailing and delivery platforms;
- traditional FMCG and food businesses;
- founder / investor decision-making across these models.

The goal is **not** to forecast prices or provide investment advice.  
The goal is to make the economics visible with compact, data-driven arguments
that founders, operators and investors can use to reason about capital allocation.

All numbers are **order-of-magnitude estimates** based on public financial reports,
sector studies and policy papers cited inside each working paper.  
Scenarios describe what *could* happen under explicit assumptions, not what
*will* necessarily occur.

---

## Files in this repository

- `paper1-ai-economics-and-adoption.md`  
  *Why AI looks like a durable general-purpose technology: investment scale,
  enterprise adoption and simple economics of AI infrastructure.*

- `paper2-blockchain-speculation-vs-utility.md`  
  *Why most blockchain activity still appears speculative, and where the small
  pocket of real-world utility may sit (mainly security and cross-border payments).*

- `paper3-ridehailing-delivery-unit-economics.md`  
  *Unit economics of ride-hailing and food-delivery platforms, with emphasis on
  contribution margins, labour constraints and regulation.*

- `paper4-fmcg-vs-tech-profitability.md`  
  *A comparison between high-growth tech/platform models and traditional FMCG / food
  companies in terms of margins, cash flow and risk.*

- `paper5-investor-founder-checklist.md`  
  *A synthetic framework and checklist for founders and investors deciding where to
  allocate scarce time and capital across AI, blockchain, platforms and “boring” businesses.*

---

## How to use this project

1. Each paper is **self-contained**. Start with `paper1` and `paper2`
   if you are primarily interested in AI vs blockchain.
2. `paper3` and `paper4` give comparative benchmarks against ride-hailing,
   delivery and FMCG businesses.
3. `paper5` is a practical summary: a checklist you can map directly into
   a memo, investment note or internal decision document.

## References and data sources

The working papers in this repository draw on publicly available data and
research from (non-exhaustive list):

### AI infrastructure, data centres and capex

- IoT Analytics – *Data Center Equipment & Infrastructure Market Report
  2025–2030* and related releases on the global data-centre infrastructure
  market (spending of ~USD 290bn in 2024 and forecast to USD 1tn by 2030).  
- Deep Market Insights – *Data Center Infrastructure Market Research
  Report* (market size ~USD 290bn in 2024, medium-term growth projections).  
- KPMG – *Future Forward: Following the Money in AI* and *2025 Futures
  Report* (hyperscaler AI capex projections and strategic context).  

### AI, productivity and adoption

- Major consulting-firm surveys on enterprise AI adoption, use cases and
  EBIT impact (McKinsey, BCG, etc.).  
- OECD and central-bank papers on AI, productivity and labour-market
  effects.  
- Experimental and firm-level studies on generative-AI productivity
  gains in coding, customer support, writing and analytical tasks.

### Blockchain, crypto and DeFi

- Bank for International Settlements (BIS) – *The crypto ecosystem: key
  elements and risks*; *Cryptocurrencies: looking beyond the hype*;
  *The future monetary system*; *Cryptocurrencies and decentralised
  finance: functions and financial stability implications*; *DeFi risks
  and the decentralisation illusion* and related FSI/BIS summaries.  
- Central-bank and international-organisation reports on crypto-asset
  usage, cross-border flows, stablecoins and tokenisation.  
- Coinbase – investor-relations quarterly and annual reports;  
  Macrotrends / Yahoo Finance statistics for Coinbase revenue and net
  income, and selected financial-press earnings coverage.

### Ride-hailing and delivery platforms

- Uber – investor-relations earnings releases and annual/quarterly
  reports (gross bookings, segment margins, adjusted EBITDA, cash flow).  
- CompaniesMarketCap, Macrotrends and similar datasets – operating-margin
  and profit-margin time series for Uber and peers.  
- Academic and policy work on platform labour markets, multi-sided
  platforms and regulation of ride-hailing and delivery.

### FMCG, food and other sector benchmarks

- NYU Stern / Aswath Damodaran – *Margins by Sector (US)* (EBIT and net
  margins by industry, including food processing, soft drinks and
  beverages).  
- Investopedia – articles summarising food-processing profit margins
  using CSIMarket data.  
- Vena Solutions – *Average Profit Margin by Industry* and similar
  cross-industry margin benchmarks.  
- Sector reports and company filings for large global FMCG and beverage
  groups.  
- Restaurant-margin benchmarks (e.g. Toast and other industry analyses)
  for context on food-service profitability.

### Cross-cutting market and macro sources

- OECD, Eurostat, World Bank and national statistical offices – macro
  data on productivity, sector value added and investment.  
- IMF and BIS publications on financial-stability risks, asset-price
  cycles and speculative dynamics.  
- Selected financial-press coverage (Financial Times, The Economist,
  MarketWatch, Reuters, etc.) for up-to-date numbers and case studies.

All numerical values in the papers are **order-of-magnitude estimates**
constructed from these sources and are intended for analytical and
educational use only.
